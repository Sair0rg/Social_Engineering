Topic 4

🎭 Pretexting

Using a false identity and believable story.

Definition

Pretexting is an attack where a criminal creates a false identity and believable story, called a pretext. The story is used to gain trust so the victim reveals information, sends money, or provides access.

How it works

The attacker may research the victim and pretend to be IT support, a police officer, manager, bank employee, teacher, or classmate. Their questions appear normal inside the invented story.

Easy example

A caller says, “I am the new IT-support worker. I need your username and password to repair your school account.” The support story is only an excuse to collect login details.

Warning signs

  • Unknown person claims an important role
  • Request for unnecessary information
  • Request must remain secret
  • Person discourages verification

How to stay safe

Do not share confidential information. Verify the identity using an official contact, teacher, supervisor, or IT team.

Notebook questions

  1. What is pretexting?
  2. What pretext is used in the example?
  3. Name two false identities attackers may use.
