Topic 8

🎁 Quid Pro Quo

Offering something in exchange for information or access.

Definition

Quid pro quo means “something for something.” In this attack, a criminal promises a service, reward, or benefit in exchange for confidential information, money, or access to a device or account.

How it works

The offer is made to feel helpful or valuable. The attacker may promise technical support, a gift, discount, or prize. After gaining information or access, they do not provide the promised benefit.

Easy example

A person says, “I will fix your slow computer for free, but first tell me your username and password.” The free help is only an excuse to collect login details.

Warning signs

  • Free help from an unknown person
  • Reward requires private information
  • Request to install remote-access software
  • Pressure to accept immediately

How to stay safe

Do not exchange private information for help or gifts. Use only trusted, officially approved support and verify offers.

Notebook questions

  1. What does quid pro quo mean?
  2. What is offered in the example?
  3. Why should passwords never be exchanged for help?
