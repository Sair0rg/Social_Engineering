💬 Smishing

Phishing through SMS, WhatsApp, or text messages.

Definition

Smishing is a social-engineering attack performed through SMS or another messaging service. The attacker sends a false message to make the victim open a link, pay money, install an app, or reveal private information.

How it works

The message usually mentions a parcel, prize, unpaid bill, bank problem, or account verification. It creates urgency and includes an unfamiliar or shortened link.

Easy example

An SMS says, “Your parcel is delayed. Pay ₹20 now using this link.” You are not expecting a parcel. The fake payment page is designed to steal card information.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected parcel or prize
  • Urgent payment request
  • Unknown or shortened link
  • Request to install an app

How to stay safe

Do not click or reply. Check the information through the company’s official app or website. Block and report the sender.
