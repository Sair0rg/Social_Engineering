Topic 1

📧 Phishing

Fake emails and websites designed to steal information.

Definition

Phishing is an attack where criminals send a fake email or create a fake website that looks genuine. They want the victim to click a harmful link, download a dangerous file, or enter a username, password, card number, or bank details.

How it works

The attacker copies the name, logo, colours, and writing style of a trusted company. The message creates fear or urgency so the victim acts before checking. Its link may open a fake login page that sends everything typed there to the attacker.

Easy example

A fake bank email says, “Your account will be blocked today. Click here and log in.” The link opens a fake bank page. When the victim types a username and password, the attacker receives them.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected urgent message
  • Strange sender address or link
  • Spelling mistakes
  • Request for a password or OTP

How to stay safe

Do not click. Check the sender and open the official app or type the real website address yourself. Report the message.

Notebook questions
