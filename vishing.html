Topic 3

📞 Vishing

Voice phishing through phone or internet calls.

Definition

Vishing is an attack where a criminal calls and pretends to represent a trusted person or organization. The caller tries to obtain an OTP, PIN, password, card number, money, or access to a device.

How it works

The caller sounds professional and may know the victim’s name. They use authority, fear, and urgency. A displayed caller number can be faked, so it does not prove the call is genuine.

Easy example

A caller says, “I am from your bank. Tell me the OTP that just arrived so I can protect your account.” The attacker actually needs the OTP to complete a transaction.

Warning signs

  • Request for OTP, PIN, or password
  • Threat of account closure
  • Demand for immediate payment
  • Caller refuses verification

How to stay safe

Never share an OTP or PIN. End the call and contact the organization using its official number.
