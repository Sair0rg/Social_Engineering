Social Engineering

Social engineering is a cyberattack method in which an attacker tricks or manipulates a person into revealing confidential information or performing an unsafe action. Instead of directly attacking a computer, the attacker targets human trust, fear, curiosity, helpfulness, or urgency.

Remember: Pause before acting, check the request using an official method, and ask a trusted person when you are unsure.
