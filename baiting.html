Topic 5

🔌 Baiting

Using curiosity, free items, gifts, or rewards.

Definition

Baiting is an attack that uses something attractive to catch a victim’s attention. The bait may be a free game, movie, prize, download, or USB drive. Accepting it can install malware or expose private information.

How it works

The attacker depends on curiosity or the desire to receive something free. Digital bait appears as a download or prize offer. Physical bait may be an infected USB left where someone will find it.

Easy example

An attacker leaves a USB labelled “Salary Details” near an office. A curious employee connects it. A harmful program on the USB infects the computer and steals files.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected free gift or prize
  • Unknown download website
  • Unknown USB in a public place
  • An offer that seems too good to be true

How to stay safe

Never connect an unknown USB or download unknown “free” files. Give found devices to authorized staff.

Notebook questions
