Topic 7

👀 Shoulder Surfing

Secretly watching private information.

Definition

Shoulder surfing happens when someone secretly watches another person’s screen, keyboard, keypad, or hand movements to learn confidential information. It can happen at an ATM, on a bus, in a classroom, or in any crowded place.

How it works

The attacker may stand nearby, watch from behind, use a camera, or observe a reflection. They try to see a PIN, password, message, card number, or other private information.

Easy example

While you enter your ATM PIN, a person behind you watches your fingers. They remember the PIN and may use it if they later obtain your card.

Warning signs

  • Someone stands unusually close
  • A person repeatedly watches your screen
  • A camera points toward a keypad
  • Private information is visible in public

How to stay safe

Cover the keypad, keep distance from others, use a privacy screen, and check your surroundings before entering private information.
